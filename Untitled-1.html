Borussia Dortmund

Founded: 19 December 1909

History

Borussia Dortmund is a professional football club based in Dortmund, Germany. The club was founded in 1909 and has since become one of the most successful clubs in German football history.

Throughout its history, Borussia Dortmund has won numerous domestic and international trophies, including multiple Bundesliga titles and UEFA Champions League.

Trophies Won

  • Bundesliga: 8 titles (last in 2021)
  • DFB-Pokal: 4 titles (last in 2017)
  • UEFA Champions League: 1 title (1997)
